# Published pieces

Status: draft.

Anyone, including an anonymous user, can read what staff have published: news, guides, and event write-ups. News is listed newest first. Guides and event write-ups might be grouped and ordered by the category and position chosen at publish time. See [Publish](../content/publish.md).

Language URLs, fallback to English, and how numbers and dates are shown are defined in [Language URLs](language-urls.md).

## Open question

This is open decision 3 in [scope](../../product/scope.md). Until it is closed, guides and event write-ups still need a category and a position, as [Publish](../content/publish.md) describes.

## Done when

- An anonymous reader can open a published news item, guide, and event write-up without signing in.
- Unpublished drafts are invisible to readers.
