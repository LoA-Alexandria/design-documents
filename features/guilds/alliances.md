# Alliances

Status: draft.

Two kinds of alliance exist.

- A permanent alliance has a member chat for accepted members of either guild, and an officer chat limited to officers of either guild.
- An event alliance is tied to one planned event. Its planning chat stays readable by both guilds, and only officers of either guild can write in it.

Who may use those chats follows [Member chat](member-chat.md) and [Officer chat](officer-chat.md).

A guild can offer an event alliance only after it has marked its own base for that event. The base is marked on the [Siege board](../events/siege-board.md) or the [Territory board](../events/territory-board.md). The invited guild sees the offer without having to open the event. Neither side can accept its own offer. Accepting turns that event on for the partnership.

Release: permanent alliances and their two chats are phase 2. Event-alliance offers wait for event planning in phase 4, because an offer carries the guild's chosen base. See [scope](../../product/scope.md).

## Done when

- A permanent alliance has a member chat for accepted members of either guild, and an officer chat only officers of either guild can use.
- An event-alliance planning chat is readable by both guilds, and only officers of either guild can write in it.
- A guild cannot offer an event alliance before its own base is marked, and neither side can accept its own offer.
- Accepting an event-alliance offer turns that event on for the partnership.
