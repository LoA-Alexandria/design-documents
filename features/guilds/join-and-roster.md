# Join and roster

Status: draft.

A signed-in member can find guilds and ask to join one. A request may include a note. Masters and officers accept or reject the request. An accepted member sets the name they want on the roster. Officers are promoted by the master, or by a site admin. The master can edit the guild's name, server, description, and icon, and can hand the master post to someone else.

A member can leave. The master cannot leave; they hand the guild on, or the listing is removed. Leaving still works when the guild is frozen. What else a freeze allows is defined in [Registration](registration.md).

Who counts as applicant, member, officer, or master is defined in [users](../../product/users.md). Chat is defined in [Member chat](member-chat.md) and [Officer chat](officer-chat.md).

Release: join, review, and roster are phase 2. Freeze behavior arrives with Premium in phase 3. See [scope](../../product/scope.md).

## Done when

- A signed-in member can request to join, and a master or officer can accept or reject that request.
- The master, or a site admin, can promote officers, edit the guild's name, server, description, and icon, and hand the master post on.
- A member can leave, including when the guild is frozen. The master cannot leave.
