# Territory board

Status: draft.

Dawn of Rome and Crown of the Nile share one territory plan, shown on one picture or its mirror. Officers paint which land and which places the guild, its allies, and its opponents hold. A place is painted as a whole. Prestige follows the painted land and places, at the rates transcribed from the game, and an unknown place shows as unknown rather than as zero. The board has no day-by-day score line; prestige is the score.

Up to four partner guilds may join this event. Each accepted partner uses one of five opponent colours, so four partners leave one colour for an enemy. Each guild in a pair holds one place, and a guild must mark its own base before it can offer the alliance. The offer itself is defined in [Alliances](../guilds/alliances.md).

## Done when

- Painting a place paints the whole place.
- Prestige uses the known rates, and an unconfirmed place does not count as zero.
- The board shows prestige rather than a day-by-day score line.
- At most four partner guilds can join, and a guild must mark its own base before it can offer the alliance.
