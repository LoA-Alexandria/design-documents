# Prepare

Status: draft.

An Editor prepares the English of a news item, guide, or event write-up. A site admin can prepare as well. A member with a language assignment fills that piece in the assigned languages and cannot change the English. A language assignment alone cannot create a piece. See [Two translation flows](../../decisions/0003-two-translation-flows.md) and [Interface strings](interface-strings.md).

News needs no category. A guide needs one of the guide categories, and an event write-up needs one of the game’s event categories. Both need a position in that category. The lists are in [Published pieces](../reading/published-pieces.md).

Preparing a piece does not make it public. Who may publish is defined in [Publish](publish.md).

## Done when

- An Editor without the publish permission can prepare the English of a piece and cannot make it public.
- An assigned member can save a translation of a piece in an assigned language and cannot change the English.
- A member with no language assignment and no Editor rank cannot prepare or translate a piece.
