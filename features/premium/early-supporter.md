# Early Supporter

Status: draft.

A site admin may award Early Supporter to a member who has active Premium. The award is one of 20 permanent places. Revoking it does not open the place again. Early Supporter does not grant, extend, or replace Premium.

The member can opt in to a public list that shows their chosen name and the award date, and nothing else. Anyone can read that list. It shows only the people who opted in.

With Discord linked, the Early Supporter and Test Version roles follow the award and remain if Premium later ends. They are removed if the award is revoked. The site mark is master. See [Stored site rank is master](../../decisions/0001-stored-rank-is-master.md).

## Done when

- A site admin can award Early Supporter only while the member has active Premium, and only while fewer than 20 places have ever been assigned.
- Revoking an award leaves that place used, and does not change the member's Premium.
- The public list shows an Early Supporter only after that member opts in, and then only their chosen name and award date.
- The Early Supporter and Test Version Discord roles follow the award for a linked Discord account, including after Premium ends, and are removed when the award is revoked.
